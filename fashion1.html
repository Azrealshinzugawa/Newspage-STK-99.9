Michelle Dee stuns in black at 2025 Love Gala

By Ron Lim | November 10, 2025

Kapuso beauty queen Michelle Dee shows everyone else how one can be fashionable while supporting a noble cause at the same time as she stunned in a black ensemble at a charity ball organized to celebrate World AIDS Day.
Held over the weekend and organized by the non-government organization (NGO) LoveYourself, the 2025 Love Gala saw the attendance of celebrities and personalities like former Miss Universe Pia Wurtzbach Jauncey, two-time Olympic gold medalist Carlos Yulo, and fashion designer Mark Bumgarner.
Michelle's attendance at the 2025 Love Gala is just one of the events where Michelle gets to combine fashion and advocacy. Michelle, alongside Bianca Gonzalez, Pia Wurtzbach Jauncey, Iza Calzado, and Angel Aquino, will also be part of Allure Philippines' inaugural Beauty Congress, which is set to happen from November 21 to November 23.
