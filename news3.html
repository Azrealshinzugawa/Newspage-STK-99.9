2 hurt after metal panel falls off billboard in Quezon City

By Jason Sigales | November 10, 2025

MANILA, Philippines — Two motorcycle riders were injured after a metal panel fell off a billboard along Katipunan Avenue amid inclement weather brought by Typhoon Uwan (international name: Fung-wong), the Quezon City Police District (QCPD) said.
The piece of debris, an aluminum composite panel, fell onto a subcompact sports utility vehicle (SUV) and a motorcycle along Katipunan Avenue southbound service road around 9:10 a.m., the QCPD said in a statement.
“The victims were immediately given first aid by responding personnel from the Bureau of Fire Protection and brought to Quirino Memorial Medical Center for medical treatment,” it added.

In a message to reporters, QCPD public information chief Maj. Jennifer Gannaban said the subcompact SUV struck by the aluminum panel was only occupied by its driver, and they were unharmed by the incident.
However, it did note that “the parties involved were able to reach an amicable settlement, and as such, no formal case will be filed.”

The development prompted the Metropolitan Manila Development Authority (MMDA) to temporarily close the segment of Katipunan Avenue southbound service road from Xavierville Avenue to Aurora Boulevard.
“Cars plying along Katipunan southbound must first go through Xavierville Avenue or the Katipunan flyover, while cars going to Marcos Highway must take the u-turn along Katipunan before B. Serrano southbound,” the MMDA said in Filipino. /mcm
